--- layout: default --- {%- comment -%} The blog index. One newest-first list of two kinds of entry: posts in _posts (on this site) and stories in _data/medium.yml (on Medium). A Medium story that a local post points at with `canonical_url` is shown once, as the local post. Each entry gets a sortable key, the keys are sorted as strings, and the entry is looked up again by source (p or m) and index when rendered. The newest entry gets a card with its cover; the rest are a compact list.
